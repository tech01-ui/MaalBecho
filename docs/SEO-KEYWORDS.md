# Maalbecho – keyword research and targeting

Research date: 2026-11-25. Method: Google autocomplete for India (a proxy for what people really type; it
does **not** show search volume) plus a look at the pages that rank for the strongest queries.
To get volumes, use Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account) and, after launch, Search Console.

## What people actually search (autocomplete, India)
| Intent | Queries seen |
|---|---|
| Manufacturer wants distributors | "how to find distributors for my product in india", "how do i find distributors for my product", "manufacturers looking for distributors" |
| Manufacturer wants more sales | "how to increase sales in manufacturing business", "how to increase revenue in manufacturing" |
| Wanting more dealers | "how to increase dealer network", "dealer network expansion", "distribution network expansion" |
| Finding buyers / customers | "how to find wholesale buyers", "how to find wholesale customers", "wholesale buyers in india", "wholesale **rice / tea / turmeric / saffron** buyers in india" |
| Hiring a service (high buying intent) | "b2b lead generation for manufacturers", "b2b lead generation companies in india" (+ **pune, mumbai, bangalore, chennai, hyderabad**), "b2b marketing agency for manufacturers in india", "b2b sales agency india", "digital marketing agency for manufacturers in india" |

## What does NOT work
- **"sales growth partner" / "sales growth agency"**: autocomplete returns only job postings and definitions. It is a brand description, not a search term, so it was demoted in titles.
- **Hinglish / Hindi** ("distributor kaise dhundhe", "dealer kaise banaye", "distributor chahiye"): almost no demand for this topic (and "dealer kaise banaye" is about property/ration dealer licences). The Hinglish headlines stay for brand voice, but keywords are English.
- **"how to become a distributor"** is people wanting a brand's distributorship, which is the opposite of our audience.

## Who ranks
Informational blogs for "find distributors in India" (trade.gov, Tradologie, Vanik, Kirana Club, IMARC), and marketplaces / agencies
(IndiaMART, Fulcrum Resources, Globpulse) for lead generation. Informational guides are the realistic way in.
