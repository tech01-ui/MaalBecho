# Images

Every picture on the site lives in `assets/img/photos/`. They are **free stock photos** used as
stand-ins until the design team delivers final artwork.

## How to replace one
Save the final image in `assets/img/photos/` with **the same file name** (e.g. `home-hero.webp`).
If you use a different extension (e.g. `.jpg` instead of `.webp`), also change that one `src` in the HTML.
Images are cropped with `object-fit: cover`, so keep the subject near the centre. Export about
1.5-2x the listed size and compress to WebP.
